How scoring works

Every lead gets a transparent 0-100 score. No black box: each point comes from a named component you can tune in scoring.json.

Tiers

Hot  Score ≥ 60. Call today - strong buying signals right now.

Warm  Score ≥ 40. Engaged or promising - follow up this week.

Cold  Everything else - keep on a nurture drip.

Leads in Pending, Closed, Past Client, Sphere or Trash are outside the active pipeline: their score is capped at 15 and they never appear on the call list. The call list always puts brand-new, uncontacted leads first regardless of score, because speed-to-lead matters more than anything else in the first hour.

Penalties: -20 if the last call outcome was "Not Interested", -15 for a bad phone number.

ComponentMax pointsWhat it measures
Engagement30Property views, saves, searches and inquiries in the last 7 days (last 48h weighted 1.5x).
Recency15How recently the lead did anything themselves - decays with a 7-day half-life-ish curve.
Responsiveness15Inbound texts/calls and 'Interested' call outcomes in the last 30 days.
Source quality10Historical quality of the lead source (referrals and open houses convert best).
Stage10Where the lead sits in your FUB pipeline.
Timeline & price10Buying timeframe, pre-approval and price point.
Follow-up gap10Engaged leads the team hasn't called/texted lately (or ever) - the 'don't drop the ball' signal.
New lead bonus12Speed-to-lead bonus for brand-new leads with no outreach yet; fades over 72h.
